## Posting tips
- Always personalize the [Name] and [cause] — copy-pasted messages get ignored.
- Include a live campaign link when possible; proof beats promises.
- Follow up after 3 days; most signups come from the first follow-up.
- On WhatsApp, keep the first message short and ask a question to start a reply.
