## Engagement & retention playbook (run alongside the calendar)

- **Pin a "How it works" post** to the top of the page for new visitors.
- **Reply to every comment within 1 hour** (FB boosts posts that get early engagement).
- **Post real campaign results weekly** — numbers beat adjectives.
- **Use live videos** (Sundays) — FB pushes live video to more feeds.
- **Boost the 3 highest-performing posts** with a small budget (K50–K100 each) targeting Lusaka/Kabwe/Ndola + "Social Impact" and "Faith-based" interests.
- **Cross-post a "campaign of the week"** to local church/NGO Facebook groups.
- **Retention:** share a monthly recap of "campaigns you helped fund" to keep donors emotionally invested.

## Posting tips for max reach
- Keep posts under 120 words; lead with the hook in the first line.
- Use 1 strong image/video (people, not logos).
- Ask a question in the last line to trigger comments.
- Always end with one clear CTA + the Play Store link.
- Use Zambian examples (school fees, boreholes, church events, medical bills) — relatability drives shares.
